Show the mechanism

When compensation becomes visible

How can a biological system look stable while an adaptation is building underneath?

One well-studied opioid mechanism gives us a concrete example. Repeated mu-opioid receptor activation can suppress part of the cell's cAMP signaling, while the cell gradually builds an opposing response. While both are present, they can partially balance one another. Removing the opioid signal can expose the adaptation that had been building underneath.
